# VibeHard EDA Workbench

Register: product.
Users: hardware engineers and makers editing schematics and PCB designs with an Agent.
Approved direction: native browser editing with backend EDA checks and export. Human edits and Agent proposals share a validated command model; edits must survive reload, support undo, and export editable engineering files.
Tone: precise, calm, compact Chinese interface. Existing VibeHard identity retained. Accessible keyboard controls, readable contrast, reduced-motion support. A real engineering canvas occupies the main viewport; marketing visuals are out of scope.
